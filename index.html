Privacy Policy

Aerta AI Customer Service

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Aerta AI Customer Service is a customer-service application designed to help businesses manage and respond to customer communications, including messages received through connected Instagram Business accounts.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how the service is configured, we may process information contained in customer messages and related Instagram communication data, such as message text, account identifiers, timestamps, and information necessary to manage a conversation.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • receive and organize customer messages;
  • help business staff review and respond to customer inquiries;
  • generate suggested responses using artificial intelligence;
  • maintain conversation context where necessary to provide the service;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application.

3. Artificial Intelligence

The application may use third-party artificial intelligence services to analyze customer messages and generate response suggestions. Information sent to such services is limited to what is necessary for the configured customer-service functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, security, analytics, or artificial intelligence functionality, subject to appropriate contractual and security measures.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain conversation history when enabled, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

To request deletion of personal information processed through the service, please contact us using the contact information below. We will review the request and take appropriate action subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

8. Instagram and Meta

The application may integrate with Instagram services provided by Meta. Instagram and Meta data is handled in accordance with applicable Meta platform requirements and the permissions granted by the connected business account.
